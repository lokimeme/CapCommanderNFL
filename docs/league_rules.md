# NFL League Rules & CBA Supplement

This document contains supplemental information on league-wide rules that impact roster construction and cap management.

## 1. Roster Composition Rules

### 1.1 The 53-Man Roster
Each team must cut down to 53 players by the end of training camp.
*   **Active/Inactive:** On gameday, only 48 players can be active.
*   **The 48th Man:** Usually must be an extra offensive lineman.

### 1.2 The Practice Squad
Allows for 16 additional players.
*   **Veteran Limit:** Up to 6 players can have unlimited accrued seasons.
*   **Protection:** Teams can 'protect' up to 4 players per week from being signed by other teams.

### 1.3 Injured Reserve (IR)
Players on IR count against the salary cap but do NOT count toward the 53-man roster limit.
*   **Return from IR:** Teams can bring back up to 8 players from IR per season.

## 2. Accrued Seasons vs. Credited Seasons

### 2.1 Accrued Seasons
Defined as 6 or more regular-season games on the active roster, IR, or PUP.
*   **Impact:** Determines free agency status (ERFA, RFA, or UFA).
    *   0-2 Accrued: Exclusive Rights Free Agent (ERFA).
    *   3 Accrued: Restricted Free Agent (RFA).
    *   4+ Accrued: Unrestricted Free Agent (UFA).

### 2.2 Credited Seasons
Defined as 3 or more regular-season games.
*   **Impact:** Determines the minimum salary tier for the player.

## 3. The 'Top 51' Rule
During the offseason, only the top 51 highest-paid players count toward the team's salary cap. This allows for training camp depth without immediate cap penalties.

## 4. Post-June 1st Designations
Teams can designate up to 2 players per year as Post-June 1st cuts. This allows them to release a player in March but treat the dead cap impact as if it happened in June (spreading the hit over two years).
